# Research contract 0.2

This additive staging contract preserves the frozen 0.1 contract, validator and gender pilot. It is not a production migration. Run the additional validator in `validate_research.py` with `contract-v0.2.json` and the repository root as the snapshot base.

The added predicates describe attributed interventions: `critiques` challenges a specified position; `revises` revises a work; `contributes_to` contributes to a bounded topic or work; `qualifies` limits a position; `assesses_scope` discusses boundaries; `proposes_programme` advocates a programme; `defines_scope` offers a definition. None means demonstrated influence or consensus. Every such claim requires its own statement, qualification, intervention year, review history, and citation-specific support assessment and access limitation. Atlas teaching umbrellas are not allowed endpoints. Provisional concepts remain distinct from accepted field classifications.

`legacy-bridge.json` adapts the two older research packets into 18 historical claims and two discovery tasks, preserving all twenty original records and citation joins. Nineteen consulted HTML/PDF witnesses become source records, not invented issued publications. Existing person IDs, compound strand addresses and complete original records are retained. The adapter does not newly accept the claims or resolve external identities.

Metadata-only evidence cannot support acceptance of an interpretive relation. A passage or abstract check is necessary but does not automatically accept a proposal. Publication/intervention dates do not supply field origins or endings: historical validity remains null without separate evidence. `fixture_only: true` and `status: staging_only` are mandatory; the original production exporter rejects these packets.
