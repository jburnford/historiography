# Request: scholars Canadian History PhDs cite most, for full treatment in the atlas

Prepared 2026-10-12 for the user and Astra. **Status: evidence and request only.** Nothing has
been applied to the atlas; entries go through reviewed revision batches.

## Decision behind this request
The atlas is for teaching historiography in Canada (user, 2026-10-12). The citation evidence
showed that the scholars Canadian History PhDs read most are largely absent from it. The user
asked to add "everyone with 50 hits or more to start". A hit is a strict History PhD,
completed 2009–2026, whose bibliography cites the person; 2009–2026 are the years in which
both large programmes (Toronto and York) are in the corpus, giving 1303 theses.

## Structure first: Canadian historiography needs group entries
Person entries need fields to belong to. The atlas has no field for Canadian historiography;
only Dickason and Howard Adams have entries. The co-citation fields of this graph
(`data/field-research/dissertation-citations-2026-10-11/summary/citation-views.html`) give
a data-driven structure. Where the Canadian historians in this list sit:
- Indigenous peoples, fur trade & the West: 22
- Liberal order, religion & the state: 16
- Postwar Canada, the Sixties & the Cold War: 15
- Moral regulation, gender, sexuality & the body: 10
- War, the military & imperialism: 10
- Quebec historiography: 9
- Immigration, race & ethnicity: 8
- Labour & the working class: 6
- Gleason · Adams · Korinek: 6
- (no clustered works): 4
- New France, early modern empire & the Atlantic: 2
- Environment, development & the state: 1
- Nationalism, memory & antimodernism: 1

A first revision could create Canadian group entries along these lines, with reviewed labels:
- liberal order and the state;
- social and labour history;
- gender, sexuality and moral regulation;
- immigration and ethnicity;
- Indigenous–settler relations, and settler colonialism (see
  `../settler-colonialism-request-2026-10-12/`);
- war, the military and politics;
- Quebec historiography;
- postwar Canada;
- environment.

The person entries would then attach to them. The current placement hints are lifts over atlas
entries whose rosters the same theses cite. For Canadian historians they mostly point to
international entries, because the rosters are international. For example, McKay's top hint is
Web history, because Ian Milligan sits in that roster. They are hints, not placements.

## Identity cleanup (`identity-decisions.csv`, `identity-review.md`)
An independent review checked the 262 person records cited by at least 40 of these PhDs:
- 229 hold one person;
- 30 mixed in works by namesakes, which are removed (A.J.P. Taylor's books from Alan Taylor,
  Lyndal Roper's from Michael Roper, Vine Deloria Jr.'s from Philip J. Deloria);
- 3 were split and are merged (J.R. Miller, John G. Reid, Colin M. Coates);
- Cole Harris's works are moved out of "Harris, Richard";
- 11 automatic Wikidata IDs were wrong; 7 are corrected and 6 missing ones were found.

The cleanup changes the list. Alan Taylor, Charles Taylor, Susan Mann (Trofimenkoff) and
Richard Harris fall below 50 once their namesakes' works are removed. Reid and Coates rise
above it, and J.R. Miller rises to 142 once his two records merge.

## Decisions for the user
1. **Order of work:** create the Canadian group entries before the people. Recommended.
2. **Batch size:** 149 entries is more than double the atlas's 60 person entries. Stage them by
   tier: 100+ (27 new), then 75–99, then 50–74.
3. **Uncertain Wikidata items:**
   - Sean Mills's item (Q89475062) conflates two people and should not be used.
   - Adele Perry's (Q64626780) is unconfirmed.
   - Cecilia Morgan, John Milloy, Brenda Macdougall and Peter A. Russell have no item. That
     matters for the people tab, not for the entries.
4. **Discipline rule:** classify people by what Canadian PhDs cite them for (the current rule),
   or by their own discipline.
5. **Theorists and international historians** (Foucault, Anderson, Said, Scott, Bourdieu,
   Stoler and the like) belong in the atlas's existing international fields. Several are
   roster-only now.
