# Canadian historians who may need person entries (proposal, 2026-10-14)

The user asked: "propose a list of Canadian historians who need an entry".

**Method.**
- **Who was considered:** every person on a Canada-family roster (plus native_newcomer,
  quebec_historiography and settler_colonialism) who has no person entry.
- **Measured by:** the number of Canadian History PhDs citing them, and readership dispersion
  across the 14 co-citation fields (`dissertation_idea_vs_content.py`).
- **Judged by:** the person-entry principle. Entries go to big-idea or framework thinkers, and
  content historians stay on rosters. A field-bound big idea counts, as Wolfe did. So does a
  historical big idea that has declined, as Innis did.

Canadian PhD counts measure Canadian reading, so they are a check only, and they never appear
in entry text. Existing person entries: McKay, Innis, Wolfe. In progress: Palmer (1.134).

## Quebec: needs its own evaluation

- **the "révisionnistes"**: Linteau, Normand Séguin;
- **Gérard Bouchard**, *Genèse des nations et cultures du Nouveau Monde* (2000);
- **Ronald Rudin**, *Making History in Twentieth-Century Quebec* (1997), on the revisionism debate.

Choose who goes front and centre from Quebec's own accounts of its historiography first (Rudin,
