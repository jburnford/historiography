# Digital history: first breadth dossier

**Partial evidence review; staging only. Two selected works do not establish field coverage through 2026.**

Digital history, computational history and digital humanities remain separate candidate identities; their relations require historical argument.

Romein and colleagues make corpus construction and changing meanings methodological issues, not simply tool choices. Milligan’s broader treatment of digitization and search provides a route to ordinary research practice. These sources support distinguishing computational techniques from broader transformations in historical work.
