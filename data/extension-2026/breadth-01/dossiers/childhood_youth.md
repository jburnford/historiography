# Childhood and youth histories: first breadth dossier

**Partial evidence review; staging only. Two selected works do not establish field coverage through 2026.**

This is a joint research dossier, not a merger: childhood and youth retain separate candidate IDs. Current selected evidence chiefly addresses childhood; existing demographic-history strands are preserved.

Tisdall connects childhood/adulthood categories with power and the limits of evidence about agency. The education article excerpt suggests a route through institutions and school-produced sources. Neither licenses treating youth as merely an older age band within a single universal developmental history.
