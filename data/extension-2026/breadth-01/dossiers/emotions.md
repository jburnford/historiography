# History of emotions: first breadth dossier

**Partial evidence review; staging only. Two selected works do not establish field coverage through 2026.**

Emotions can be a field and an analytical category across fields; programme publication dates do not mark the beginning of emotional life or its historical study.

The interview identifies several distinct analytical programmes; Scheer proposes embodied practice as another route. Their juxtaposition supplies questions about communities, regimes, norms and bodies, rather than grounds for collapsing them into one theory.
