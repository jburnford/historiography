# CHA women presidents: Neatby and Ormsby

Prepared 2026-10-12. Research only; ANSWERS.md and the drafts are unchanged. Structured version: `cha-women-presidents.json`.

## Answer (confidence: high)
- **Hilda Neatby** was the first woman president of the Canadian Historical Association, in **1962–1963**. Her presidential address in 1963 was "Jean-Olivier Briand: A 'Minor Canadien'".
- **Margaret A. Ormsby** was the **second**, in **1965–1966**. Her address in 1966 was "A Horizontal View".
- Marcel Trudel (1963–64) and Mason Wade (1964–65) served between them.
- No woman appears in the CHA's list before Neatby, from L. J. Burpee (1922–25) onward. The next woman after Ormsby was Margaret E. Prang (1976–77).

## The conflict in Q89
The Governor General honours record (https://www.gg.ca/en/honours/recipients/146-5300, cited in ANSWERS.md and not re-fetched here) calls Ormsby the "first woman president". The CHA's own list contradicts this. BC Book Look's "second female president" agrees with the CHA.

**Suggested wording:** "Margaret Ormsby was president of the Canadian Historical Association in 1965–66, its second woman president after Hilda Neatby (1962–63)."
