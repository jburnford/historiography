## Decisions

- **Male focus.** The brief asked about criticism of his focus on male, English-Canadian, central-Canadian historians.
  - No in-period text found here faults *Writing* for its all-male subjects. Trofimenkoff (1979) criticises the field and
    *Sense of Power*'s imperialists; the draft says so and does not overstate.
  - Mills names eight "superluminaries".
- **Quebec omission.** The draft presents it as Berger's stated choice (preface; p. 181) and records Rudin's in-period
  criticism.
- **Central Canada.** No regional critique of *Writing* was found.
- **Founder claims.** The draft makes none. Monet credits Penlington (1965) with suggesting the thesis.
- **Careless misattribution.** Terry Cook (1977) cites the "nation-building … neglects and obscures" passage as
  Berger's (p. 263). The 1986 text shows that Berger is quoting Careless (1969). The draft attributes it to Careless.
- **No citation counts in the text.** Citation counts are in `citation_evidence` only.
