## Summary
1. I reviewed 17 edges and re-read the primary texts: Simpson 2007, Trask 1991 and Andersen's 2014 introduction in full, and Coulthard's 2009 dissertation by full-text search with the needed pages read. 8 edges are kept, 9 revised and 0 rerouted.
2. Errors found: every Coulthard dissertation locator is one page too high, and the edge to `native_newcomer` (edge_2164) pins a phrase Andersen aimed at John Ralston Saul on historians. There are no misattributed targets.
3. Missing strands: Simpson's and Coulthard's `settler_colonialism` edges should carry `recognition_refusal_resurgence`, and Trask's settler-colonialism edge should carry `indigenous_scholars_critique` (or `earlier_settler_societies`). There is still no read text on any of the four as a critic of Wolfe or of the field.
4. I propose 6 new edges: Andersen → `ethnohistory` (critique, 2011 abstract), Andersen → Taylor (critique), Bourdieu → Andersen, Deloria → Coulthard, Howard Adams → Coulthard, and Harris → Coulthard. A Foucault → Andersen edge is optional.
5. Distinct positions are kept apart. Coulthard rejects the politics of recognition, Simpson refuses, and Andersen keeps Bourdieuvian (mis)recognition while criticising Taylor's concept of identity. Trask answers Keesing's invention thesis; her critique of historians needs *From a Native Daughter*, which is held.
