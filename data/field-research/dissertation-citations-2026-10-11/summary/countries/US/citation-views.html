American History PhDs: fields, supervision and universities

Built from the bibliographies of 1,979 History doctoral dissertations (strict History PhDs with a parsed bibliography). Every measure is a share of dissertations, so corpus growth and department size do not read as influence.

1 · Fields: what is cited together

Each dot is a work cited by at least 15 History PhDs (734 works; corporate authors and works cited mainly as primary sources excluded). Works close together are cited by the same dissertations. Fields are the consensus of 40 clustering runs: works stay in a field only if they fall together in at least 80% of runs (694 of 734 works). Field names are provisional editorial labels. Select a field to see its works and people; click a dot to see a work's co-citation neighbours and whether it bridges fields. Robustness grades come from re-clustering at other resolutions and consensus thresholds (audit/countries/US/field-robustness.json).

How fields change: share of each year's dissertations engaging the field

A dissertation engages a field when it cites at least three of its works. Centred three-year window, 95% band; grey = fewer than 30 dissertations in the window.

4 · Practice: what bibliographies are built from

Share of each year's dissertations whose bibliography has a section of each kind, read from the dissertation's own subheadings (1,755 History PhDs with a subheaded bibliography). Headings follow local conventions, so read levels cautiously and changes with more confidence. Centred three-year window, 95% band; grey = thin window.
