Stability is the mean match across bootstrap refits (1 = always reproduced).

Notes and limits

  • Only strict History PhDs count; non-History and unparsed theses are excluded.
  • Works are resolved citation records; a few title variants remain unmerged, so counts are slight undercounts.
  • The United Kingdom corpus leans towards universities with open repositories and towards the 2010s; several of the largest programmes are missing (see the weighted section). Families cover only theses that cite at least 3 widely cited works, which under-represents older theses.
  • Source data: works.csv, cited-people.csv, dissertations.csv and summary/countries/<CC>/tfidf-families/.
