  • Links join works co-cited by at least 5 theses, weighted by association strength (co-citations relative to what the two works' citation counts would give by chance, as in VOSviewer); each work keeps its 5 strongest, 774 in all. Position comes from a spring layout of these links; the axes mean nothing. Works linked only to each other, apart from the main map, are left out: Les femmes ou les silences de l’histoire, Écrire l’histoire des femmes et du genre, Kungamakt och bonderätt. Om danska kunga, Nationen på spel. Kropp, kön och svenskh.
  • Colour: a work leans to a region when that region's share of History PhDs citing it is at least 1.5 times the next region's. Europe here is all European countries together, France included.
  • Works are first-author surname and main title, with translations mapped through Wikidata counted as one work. A citation is not engagement.
