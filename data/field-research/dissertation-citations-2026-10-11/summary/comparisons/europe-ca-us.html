How to read this

  • Sources. Strict History PhDs (explicit History programme or department) with a parsed bibliography or a bibliography rebuilt from their notes. British theses come from UK repositories (the OpenAIRE collection and Oxford's ORA). French and other European theses come mostly from the BASE harvest, where non-English bibliographies parse less cleanly than English ones, so their shares are, if anything, low.
  • Works. A work is first-author surname plus main title. Translations are merged only where Wikidata maps them to one work: some remain split (Weber's French Économie et société, Foucault's French Histoire de la sexualité), which lowers those works' shares outside their language.
  • People. First authors only; a person's share counts theses citing any of their works.
  • Citation is not engagement. A citation can be a frame, a foil or a ritual reference.
