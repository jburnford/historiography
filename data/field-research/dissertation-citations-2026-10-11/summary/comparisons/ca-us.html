Notes and limits

  • Only strict History PhDs count; non-History and unparsed theses are excluded.
  • Works are resolved citation records; a few title variants remain unmerged, so counts are slight undercounts.
  • The United States corpus is weighted towards universities with open repositories; elite private departments (Yale, Chicago, Columbia, Princeton) are scarce. Families cover only theses that cite at least 3 widely cited works, which under-represents older theses.
  • Source data: works.csv, cited-people.csv, dissertations.csv and summary/countries/<CC>/tfidf-families/.
