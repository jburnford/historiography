Schmitt and Moreira receive credit for their own arguments; review questions are not automatically assigned to interviewees.

Brown’s 1997 acknowledgment and 2012 criticism are different claims. Le Goff’s positive spatial debt to Braudel coexists with a qualified objection to immobility; his praise of Duby’s Bouvines coexists with criticism of Duby’s presentation of women. Ariès agrees on the cultural turning point while questioning neglected older beliefs and revising his own dating. These are not blanket rejections.
