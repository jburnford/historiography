# Research holds and boundaries

This register preserves leads that do **not** authorize an additional edge in the current proposal. Some require a missing personal endpoint; others require primary reading, identity resolution, a narrower attribution or evidence that a separate edge adds a distinct claim. Holds are not rejections of a scholar’s importance and do not commission new biographies.

Repeated case references have been consolidated while retaining their node-specific qualifications and originating references. Different claims involving the same pair remain separate when their evidence limits differ. The register includes explicit held cases and node-specific research questions; its count is not a count of missing graph relationships.

## Priorities and safeguards

- **Absent endpoints:** Limerick/New Western History, Bolton, George Wilson Pierson, Billington and several named women’s/frontier critics; Bloch’s Pirenne, Maitland and Simiand. Ruth Roach Pierson is a different person. Do not replace an unavailable critic with an entire field.
- **Claims requiring primary reading:** examples include Harvey–Lefebvre, Bhabha–Fanon, Hall–Williams and particular Ricoeur responses. A title or publisher description supplies a lead, not a verified intellectual arrow.
- **Attribution and collaboration:** retain Linda Kealey’s joint credit with Iacovetta. Davidoff–Hall and Stoler–Cooper require the actual joint framing for the proposed substantive collaboration claim. Bibliographic certainty alone is insufficient for that argument.
- **Overbroad or anonymous criticism:** Greer’s qualified treatment of Wolfe does not justify a blanket personal attack. Johnson deliberately leaves an agency example unnamed. No Berlin attribution follows from speculation.
- **Already represented or mixed records:** Benson’s Turner/Beard claim and the existing multi-voice field critiques remain intact. Named personal routes may improve visibility without making the argument newly discovered. The Johnson–Genovese cross-lane proposals require consolidation, not two counts of the same critique.
