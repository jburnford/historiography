# Historical chronology pilot — 2026-10-15

Research candidate for 14 graph entries and two journals; **not applied to the graph or site**.

Historical decisions are authored in [pilot-decisions.json](pilot-decisions.json). [pilot.json](pilot.json) preserves the baseline, source records and reading limits; [pilot-summary.csv](pilot-summary.csv) supports comparison. See [pilot contract](pilot-contract.md) for the meaning of each field.

The event sets are a minimum for prototyping, not a replacement for every existing work or strand. Selected ranges are computed from contribution events only. Context, editions on a separate track and reception cannot silently extend them. These ranges do not assert careers, complete field histories or periods of influence.

New external checks are limited to the observations and access limits recorded in the decision file. Other support reuses the existing graph’s explicitly qualified source notes; this pilot does not claim to have reread those works.
