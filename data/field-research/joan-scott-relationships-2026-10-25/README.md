## Thompson: resource and critique

Scott’s own 2009 memoir, printed p 35, describes following the lead of Thompson’s Making to make workers’ protest politically intelligible. Her 1991 Evidence of Experience, printed 784–786, critiques the foundational status of experience and its treatment of difference while acknowledging his transformative achievement. Her 2024 interview further credits his cultural account of class formation while questioning the treatment of sexual difference in language. This is a separately dated clarification, not proof of a change of mind. Both directions remain visible.
