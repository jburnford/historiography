# Revision 1.139: seminar pathways

The user asked for these changes on 2026-10-14:
- **Meaning, language, and historical evidence.** Add Wittgenstein. He now opens the pathway, with
  a new question on meaning in use and shared practice, set against Saussure's system of signs and
  White's form.
- **Caribbean, African and South Asian challenges to historical explanation**, formerly
  "Caribbean and African…". The user: "we could add subalterns and provincializing Europe from
  India to this". It adds Subaltern Studies, Ranajit Guha and Dipesh Chakrabarty, with a revised
  comparison question and a new question on Subaltern historians' critique of elite histories of
  Indian nationalism.
- **The question of class** (new). The user: "it should start with Thompson's turn against vulgar
  marxism"; and "Think about Joy Parr for the class question". The pathway runs:
  1. Thompson's turn against economistic Marxism (ep_thompson → karl, 1.134);
  2. Dobb, Dorothy Thompson, Althusser and Hobsbawm;
  3. the 1987 Scott–Palmer exchange;
  4. Joy Parr's *Gender of Breadwinners*;
  5. the languages of class;
  6. Chakrabarty and Subaltern Studies;
  7. intersectionality;
  8. Canadian working-class history and the liberal order debate.

  It has seven questions and an exercise reading one source four ways: Thompson, Scott, Palmer
  and Parr.
