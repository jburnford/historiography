The graph extension separates these collections:

- `nodes`: candidate periodicals and publication subjects. Historical-source magazines are typed separately from research journals. Databases and multi-title collections belong in `discovery_resources`.
- `edges`: only `founded_for`, `site_of_debate`, and `principal_venue`, directed from journal to an existing atlas field, programme or debate. Each carries a specific claim, evidence and dates. Degree does not measure quality.
- `subject_classifications`: checked directory, library, publisher or index classifications, plus explicitly provisional title indications. These support filtering and coverage research, not drawable edges.
- `classification_reviews`: candidate-level outcomes, including unresolved titles with reasons and next steps.
- `title_relationships`: bibliographic predecessor/successor metadata. A title change is distinct from a publication closing.
- `sources`: locally scoped provenance records, independent of the original atlas bibliography.

Schema 1.1 preserves classification status and evidence scope. Title indications have `status: provisional`, `checked_on: null` and a review date; do not present them as checked publisher remit. Earlier source classifications without an explicit status remain checked within their recorded scope. CSV exports use separate checked and provisional subject columns.

Only files listed with matching SHA-256 hashes in `accepted-subject-batches.json` are imported. Accepted files must remain unchanged. Source research is staged separately under `source-check-batches/`, accepted by hash, and applied after the title pass. A successful check can explicitly supersede provisional classification IDs; original records remain in `superseded_subject_classifications` and previous outcomes in `classification_review_history`. Active counts/CSV exclude superseded rows. Failed checks preserve current evidence and record attempts. This is journal schema 1.2; current publisher scope must not be projected across historical title changes. The initial accepted review covers the frozen 1,297 candidates that lacked classifications at revision 1.106.

No impact factors enter the graph or its prominence rules. The user’s approximate figures remain solely in the local raw OOIR transcription. That raw directory is excluded from public builds.
