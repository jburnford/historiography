**Read complete Dobb reply pp. 157–167 using OCR**, and **Sweezy pp. 134–137**. Other Sweezy passages surfaced from cached text, but do not claim the full critique read. Original Studies (1946) remains independently unread.

- Dobb pp. 157–158 defines feudalism through coercive producer–overlord relations and dominant extraction, allowing different service/kind/money forms and incidental other relations. Explicit Capital III use, not only a generic Marxist label.
- Pp. 159–161: no simple automatic peasant revolt → capitalism. Internal conflict and external trade interact, with priority to internal relations shaping the effect. Urban attraction and exploitation can both explain flight; a threat of flight can force concessions. Cheap hired labour and differentiation affect whether trade erodes or strengthens coercion. Do not flatten into a binary rejection of trade. His generalized Asia/stationarity assertions are situated claims, not adopted facts.
- Pp. 162–164: transitional petty production does not by itself define a separate mode; asks who rules and how the state is organized. His English-revolution/class interpretation is a contested position. P. 163 explicitly refers to debate on Hill’s English Revolution 1640 pamphlet; primary Hill text not read here.
- Pp. 164–165: names **Tawney**, **Kosminsky**, **Unwin** as resources on rising producers, peasantry and guild changes. Unwin attribution is explicitly tentative memory. Concrete incoming connections possible, with specified limitations. Names Nef’s patentees in contrast. Do not claim all cited works independently consulted.
- Pp. 166–167 **concedes weak evidence for the realization phase** while retaining a hypothesis. Colonial wealth financing industrialization is proposed for investigation, not established. Preserve self-correction rather than asserting a proven colonial-loot causal chain.
- Sweezy pp. 134–137 challenges the definition’s breadth and unexamined regional transfer; his production-for-use system does not mean no money or no instability. He argues instability need not transform relations. Explicitly uses Marx/Engels and Pirenne. His empirical/Asia claims also require attribution. Need selected later critique on money/trade and realization plus Dobb reception beyond this exchange if possible.
