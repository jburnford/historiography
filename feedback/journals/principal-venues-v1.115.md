# Principal journals and atlas gaps — revision 1.115

## Result

With thirteen unsupported founding claims withdrawn, the catalogue has **39 venue edges: 27 principal, nine founding and three debate**. Specific venue links now reach 24 of the 66 group entries. The inventory remains 1,632 title candidates.

All 66 group entries receive a target-fit screen below. This is a completed initial screen, not a completed journal-history review or proof that the most important journals are comprehensively represented. Shortlisted titles are discovery leads; accepted links have separate evidence.

## Selection method

- Principal means an important selected venue, not uniquely most important. No minimum or maximum quota.
- Require sustained historical or institutional role; current remit, title match, founding year and one isolated famous article are insufficient.
- Edge intervals delimit the reviewed role, not the field’s lifespan. 2000 is a coverage boundary.
- Full text, extracts, abstracts and participant retrospectives retain distinct access/attribution qualifications.
- A missing principal edge is an evidence/target-fit gap, not evidence that a field lacked important journals.
- Book series and political/primary-source periodicals do not become research-journal nodes merely to fill a field.

The evidence favours retrospective accounts of actual contributions and institutional roles. Some are participant accounts, and two selections have explicit partial-access limits: Censer’s JSH abstract and a search-indexed WHA tribute for JWH (direct PDF unavailable). These are identified in the data, not presented as full-text checks. Institutional significance is an editorial selection rationale, not a numerical prestige rank.

## Founding reappraisal

The thirteen pending 1.113 claims have been resolved for this revision: retain **Isis** with the 1912/1913 distinction, withdraw the other twelve founding inferences, and accept separately sourced principal roles where justified. **History and Anthropology** and **Holocaust and Genocide Studies** remain venue-research leads; first-volume reviews do not establish sustained importance. The earlier **Journal of World History** founding inference is also withdrawn in favour of consolidation. The previously withdrawn Environment and History claim stays withdrawn.

The complete earlier claims and reasons are preserved in the immutable correction batch and snapshot; IDs are not recycled. Other retained founding edges keep their original qualifications. This is not a new worldwide founding-journal census.

## Atlas gaps to develop

The strongest dedicated-entry candidates remain **public history, medical/health history and urban history**. This carries forward the sourced [1.112 gap review](founding-and-gaps-v1.112.md); current link counts are not new proof of those gaps. Existing strands already cover parts of each field. A new entry needs a historical account and relationships to those strands before journal edges can target it.

Broader intellectual history/history of ideas and comparative genocide studies remain narrower target-fit questions. No new field nodes are added here.
