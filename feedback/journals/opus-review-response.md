# Journal-specific response to Opus

Read the updated `opusreview.md` on 2026-09-13, including the revised edge proposal, and applied the user’s explicit direction. Other review issues are deferred.

The inventory remains broad. Unclassified, non-Western and sparsely connected journals are retained. Neither degree nor impact factor controls inclusion or prominence. Source categories, region studied, language of publication, publisher location and intellectual affiliation are different facts.

The visual relation vocabulary is now `founded_for`, `site_of_debate`, `principal_venue`. Generic publishing links were removed before integration. The 371 directory/publisher subject associations remain classification metadata. Founding requires an evidenced programme, not just a date; debate links name publications a student can locate; principal-venue claims require a sustained period and an editorial selection rationale. No principal-venue claim has yet passed that longitudinal review.

Four examples have evidence: Journal of Social History → social history (1967); History Workshop Journal → History Workshop (1976); the original Annales d’histoire économique et sociale → the Annales founding programme (1929); Past & Present → the Stone–Hobsbawm narrative exchange (1979–1980). The evidence notes distinguish full-page, abstract and bibliographic checks.
